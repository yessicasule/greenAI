      acknowledged in `paper/draft.md`'s threats-to-validity section.

      `paper/results.md` with a PASS verdict.

      PENDING/UNVERIFIED state that appears stated as fact in the paper.
- [ ] `paper-writer` invoked `verifier` on every section before it was
      marked final (not just drafted).
